Privacy Policy

8bits2infinity Channel Agent — last updated November 7, 2026

8bits2infinity Channel Agent ("the app") is a private tool operated by Minty Fresh Collectibles LLC to manage its own YouTube channel, @8bits2infinity. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

With the signed-in account's permission (Google OAuth, YouTube scope), the app reads and updates that account's own YouTube channel data: video titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, playlists, publishing schedules and basic channel analytics.

How data is used

Data is used only to organize and publish the channel owner's own videos. The app does not access any other user's account, does not sell or share data with third parties, and does not use data for advertising.

Storage

The OAuth sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. Video metadata drafts are kept as working files by the channel owner. Nothing is stored on a public server.

Google API Services

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube data is handled under the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
